# Umrah Pack PWA

A small, installable packing checklist that works offline after the first visit. It stores checked items, custom items, and notes in the browser on your device.


## Install on your phone
Open the published HTTPS link on your phone.
- **Android / Chrome:** use the browser menu and choose **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.
- **iPhone / Safari:** tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.

Open it once while online so the app files can be cached for offline use.

## Notes
- Checklist data is stored locally in the browser on that device; it does not sync between devices.
- Clearing browser site data may erase your progress, so keep a separate copy of important travel details.
- GitHub Pages sites are public. Do not store passport numbers, passwords, or other sensitive information in the app notes.
- The included checklist is a general reminder list, not official travel or religious guidance. Confirm current requirements with your airline and official sources.
